# App and site names Pseudo never masks in window titles (M8).
# They are split out before detection, so "New Tab - Google Chrome" isn't read as a person.
# Everything else in a title still goes through full detection.
#
# Rules for adding a name:
#   - Use the full form that appears in titles ("Visual Studio Code", not "Code").
#   - Never add anything that could be a person's name on its own. That's why the Claude
#     and Hermes desktop apps are NOT here: their product names are just "Claude" and
#     "Hermes" (checked from the .exe metadata), which are also first names.
#   - A name glued to @ . / - or letters is not exempted ("notepad@okaxis" is still masked).
# Upper/lower case doesn't matter. Everything after a # is a comment.
